## 🌟 Regen Bazaar: fund real-world impact

Regen Bazaar is a marketplace for **tRWI, Tokenized Real-World Impact**: a token for a verified impact report, split into editions that many funders can own.

NGOs and community groups describe the good they did. Regen Bazaar scores it with a published formula, a person reviews it, the claim is recorded onchain with EAS, and anyone can fund it in a stablecoin. The organisation is paid in the same transaction.

**Status:** public beta on testnets.

| Pilot | Network | What it funds |
|---|---|---|
| [Clean Phangan](https://cleanphangan.regenbazaar.com) | Optimism | Weekly beach and jungle cleanups on Koh Phangan: 205+ operations, 90+ tons of waste collected, 5,000+ participants |
| [EcoThailand Foundation](https://ecothailand.regenbazaar.com) | Celo | Mangrove and coral restoration, community gardens and sustainability education in the Thai Gulf |
